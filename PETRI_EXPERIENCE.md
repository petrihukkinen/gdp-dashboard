- P&L responsibility across multi-country service operations.
- Approximately 43 Full Service maintenance contracts in Finland developed *towards*

Petri personally. **What it is not evidence for:** whether all 43 contracts reached

sell a fixed-price/performance deal (ABB) and the party deciding whether to buy one and how
much control to give up (Neste, and as co-author of AMOR, effectively both — Neste

in a deliverable, cite the specific mechanism it supports (e.g. "customers resist ceding
contractor selection — Petri experienced this from the Neste side on AMOR") rather than
general authority.
