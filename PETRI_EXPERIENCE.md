# Petri Hukkinen — Relevant Experience (Level B unless noted)

This is not a CV. It exists to identify which claims in this project are backed by direct
professional experience (Level B) versus general market hypothesis (Level C), and to keep
that experience from being universalized into market fact.

## Service provider side — ABB (Level B, per project brief)
- International industrial service and maintenance leadership.
- P&L responsibility across multi-country service operations.
- Approximately 43 Full Service maintenance contracts in Finland developed from
  traditional hourly-based maintenance towards fixed-price and performance-oriented models.
- Later led Full Service business in Asia.

**What this is evidence for:** the mechanics of moving a maintenance book of business from
time-and-materials to fixed-price/performance pricing — deal structuring, margin risk,
customer resistance patterns. **What it is not evidence for:** whether outcome-based /
gainshare models work at scale across industries beyond what was actually contracted in
those 43 deals — the project brief does not specify how many of the 43 reached full
performance/gainshare pricing versus fixed-price only.

**What this is evidence for:** how an asset owner evaluates outsourcing risk, what an

## Why the dual perspective matters
Petri has sat on both sides of the maintenance-outsourcing negotiation: the party trying to
sell a fixed-price/performance deal (ABB) and the party deciding whether to buy one and how
much control to give up (Neste, and as co-author of AMOR, effectively both — Neste
asset-management leadership proposing to become the outsourced provider). This is a
genuinely unusual vantage point and is the strongest asset-specific credibility this project
has. It should be used as evidence, not autobiography — every time this experience is cited
in a deliverable, cite the specific mechanism it supports (e.g. "customers resist ceding
contractor selection — Petri experienced this from the Neste side on AMOR") rather than
general authority.
