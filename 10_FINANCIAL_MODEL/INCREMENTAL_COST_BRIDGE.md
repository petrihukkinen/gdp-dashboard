# Incremental Cost Bridge — Cycle 11

The relevant question is not "what does the Control Tower cost" — it is **what is the
incremental cost of the AMOR accountability layer compared with a high-quality conventional
integrated maintenance contract?**

## Conventional contract operating layer (the baseline, Category A)
Site Manager, Work Management Lead, most of Reliability Lead, most of Commercial
Controller, most of Contract Director. **This work exists and is paid for regardless of
whether AMOR's accountability architecture is adopted.** Charging the AMOR model for this
work as if it were new would be a real accounting error this bridge exists to prevent.

## AMOR accountability operating layer — the bridge

| Line | Nature | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Existing cost (Category A) | Already in the conventional baseline | `WORK_CLASSIFICATION.md` |
| Transferred cost (Category C) | Moves from customer to provider — real cost to the provider, but *not new to the system*, and may come with corresponding transferred contract value (AMOR 2020 precedent: ~350 FTE transfer, `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` A2) | `CUSTOMER_DUPLICATION_TEST.md` |
| **Incremental cost (Category B)** | **Genuinely new**: Contractor Integration Lead's formal authority function, Event 4 evidence discipline, accountability-boundary/governance participation | `ZERO_BASED_CONTROL_TOWER_ORG.md` |
| Avoided duplicate cost | A customer-side benefit (reduced/redeployed internal coordination cost) — strengthens the system-level case but does not reduce the provider's own cost line | `CUSTOMER_DUPLICATION_TEST.md` |
| Shared cost | Commercial Controller, Data Analyst, VVB support — pooled across accounts under Model B/C, undiluted under Model A | `CONTROL_TOWER_OPERATING_MODELS.md` |

## The critical finding
Much of what earlier cycles' governance sketches (the "Asset Performance Office" vision in
`08_OPERATING_MODEL/README.md`) implied as new Control Tower cost is actually **Category A
(already exists in any competent conventional contract) or Category C (transferred, not
created)**. The genuinely new incremental cost is narrower than assumed — concentrated in
the Contractor Integration Lead's formal authority and the governance/evidence discipline
specifically required by Event 4 and any active optional module.

This is good news for the risk Cycle 10 flagged (Control Tower operating cost as the
thesis's most dangerous variable) — **but only if the organizational discipline in
`WORK_CLASSIFICATION.md` is actually followed in real delivery, not just on paper.** That
discipline is a real execution risk, not a given — see `CONTROL_TOWER_COST_RED_TEAM.md`,
item 1.
