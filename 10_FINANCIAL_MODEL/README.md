# 10_FINANCIAL_MODEL

Gate 3 Commercial Viability modelling (Cycle 5). Read in this order:

1. `modelling_assumptions.md` — illustrative site, event-value inputs, Cycle 4/5 numeric
   decisions applied, gainshare split assumption, cash-flow settlement options. **Every
   number here is a labeled modelling assumption, not real customer or market data.**
2. `model_A_base_fee_kpi_fee.md`, `model_B_atomic_value_share.md`,
   `model_C_control_tower_value_modules.md` — the three commercial archetypes, each with
   revenue architecture, provider economics, customer economics, and cash-flow modelling.
3. `model_comparison_and_stress_test.md` — side-by-side comparison and 8 risk-corridor stress
   scenarios, including MII band sensitivity (±10%/15%/20%).
4. `cfo_tests_and_claims_bureaucracy.md` — customer and provider CFO question-by-question
   answers, plus the claims-bureaucracy burden estimate and a proposed simplified claims
   tier.
5. `commercial_red_team.md` — 10 commercial failure scenarios, explicitly not defending the
   architecture.
6. `gate3_verdict.md` — Checker verification (including a disclosed Maker arithmetic
   inconsistency the Checker caught) and the formal verdict: **PASS WITH CONDITIONS.**
