# AMOR 2030 — Project Rules (Non-Negotiable)

This file governs all work in this repository. It is binding on every future session.

## What this project is

A CEO-level strategy build for Petri Hukkinen, testing whether a large industrial services
company can move from maintenance delivery to measurable asset performance outcomes
("Asset Performance as a Service" — working name, not confirmed). The project originates
from AMOR (Asset Management One Refinery), a real 2020 management buy-out proposal at
Neste's One Refinery (Porvoo/Naantali), documented in `01_SOURCE_MATERIAL/`.

## Rules

1. This is a CEO-level industrial services strategy project. Every deliverable must survive
   contact with a skeptical CFO, a skeptical customer, and a Red Team — not just read well.
2. Commercial credibility is more important than presentation quality.
3. Do not produce generic consulting language. If a sentence could appear in any strategy
   deck for any industry, cut it or make it specific to industrial maintenance/asset
   performance economics.
4. Do not invent facts. Do not invent AMOR 2020 content beyond what is in the source PDF.
   Do not invent Petri's credentials beyond what he has told us. Do not invent Bilfinger
   capabilities, competitor weaknesses, or customer statements.
5. Separate every material claim into Level A (verified fact, sourced), Level B (Petri's
   direct professional experience), or Level C (strategic hypothesis requiring validation).
   See `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`.
6. Customer economics come before provider capabilities. Every value pool starts from what
   it costs the customer, not from what the provider wants to sell.
7. AI is an enabler, not the strategy. No AI use case is included without passing the AI
   Value Test (problem, decision, data, availability, quality, owner, integration, human,
   value, measurement, scale).
8. Total plant OEE (or plant availability) must never be treated as provider-controlled
   without an explicit attribution framework separating maintenance-caused loss from
   operations, process technology, feedstock, and customer-controlled Capex.
9. Gainshare requires objective, jointly-governed value validation. The provider never
   unilaterally calculates its own gainshare.
10. Painshare must be capped, collared, and bounded by an explicit maximum annual exposure.
    Never recommend unlimited performance liability.
11. Avoid KPI inflation. Only a small number of KPIs may affect payment; most KPIs are
    context or management KPIs.
12. Do not double-count value pools. A euro of savings claimed under "hands-on-tool-time"
    cannot also be claimed under "contractor management" without an explicit allocation rule.
13. The model must be scalable beyond one customer/one site. A bespoke consulting engagement
    that cannot be replicated is not a scalable service business — say so if that's where the
    evidence points.
14. Every major deliverable passes Maker → Checker → Red Team before it is presented as done.
15. Maintain `RISK_REGISTER.md` live.
16. Maintain `HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md` live.
17. Maintain `DECISION_LOG.md` live — one entry per material strategic decision, with the
    date, the decision, and the reasoning.
18. Preserve Petri Hukkinen's authentic industrial leadership voice — direct, numbers-first,
    unsentimental about people and cost. Not consultant-speak.
19. Do not turn this project into a job application or a CV exercise.
20. Do not create polished PowerPoint slides or an executive deck until the strategic
    architecture has passed Gate 3 (Commercial Viability). See `WORKPLAN.md` for gate status.

## Key naming note

The original AMOR source material refers to the customer as "OPP" and "One Refinery"
(Porvoo/Naantali) and to the parent company as Neste (Neste's logo appears on the closing
slide, and later slides say "Neste and AMOR" directly). Treat "OPP" as Neste's refining
organisation — this is inferred from internal consistency of the document, not stated
outright as an equivalence anywhere in the deck, so it is logged as Level A-inferred, not
Level A-stated. See `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`.
