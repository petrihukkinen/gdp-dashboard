Theory with bolometric flux L/(4πD²(1+z)^p): catalog-frame m_R = M_Q + 5log10(D/10pc) + 2.5·p·log10(1+z) + K_QR.
Standard FRW: p=2 (energy + rate). Adding a *further* 5log10(1+z) 'for the K-correction' counts (1+z)^2 twice.
The only (1+z) inside K itself is the bandwidth term (-2.5 log10(1+z) in the f_nu/AB convention), which SALT2/
SNANA handle in the model integration; it is not the energy/arrival-rate dilution.
