# Project Context — Bilfinger Asset Performance 2030

## Who and why

Petri Hukkinen, M.Sc. Engineering, 30+ years in industrial maintenance, asset management, and performance-based service business. This project supports a senior leadership recruitment process with Bilfinger. The output is a CEO-level business growth concept, built to demonstrate how Petri's combined experience — service-provider side (ABB) and asset-owner side (Neste) — translates into a credible next-stage commercial strategy for Bilfinger, not a rehash of a past deal (AMOR) and not a slide deck about Petri.

## Petri's experience base (fact — lived experience, not hypothesis)

**ABB — international maintenance/service leadership.** Led Full Service / maintenance-outsourcing business. In Finland, ~43 maintenance contracts were migrated from hourly-based models toward fixed-price and performance-based models. Later led Full Service business in Asia. This is service-provider-side experience: what it takes to sell, price, and deliver performance contracts, and where a provider's commercial risk actually bites.

**Neste — Director-level Asset Management.** Responsibility spanning refineries, terminals, and a major industrial asset base. ISO 55000 / strategic asset management, reliability, lifecycle management, maintenance, shutdowns, contractors, and investment planning. This is asset-owner-side experience: what a customer actually needs, what they will and won't hand over, and where an owner's trust breaks.

**AMOR (2020) — see AMOR_ANALYSIS.md.** A management buy-out concept developed with four Neste colleagues (Hukkinen, Ala-Nikkola, Raassina, Kekkonen) to spin Neste's One Refinery maintenance/asset-management function (~350 FTE) into an independent integrator company. Full source deck extracted in `01_SOURCE_MATERIAL/AMOR_DECK_TRANSCRIPT.md`.

## What this project explicitly is not

- Not a job-application presentation about Petri.
- Not a generic consulting deck (no "leverage synergies" language).
- Not an AI-buzzword pitch — digital/AI content must clear the bar in `07_DIGITAL_AI/` or it doesn't appear.
- Not a revival of the AMOR MBO. AMOR proves the underlying capability and pattern-recognition; it is not the product.

## The working concept

**Bilfinger Asset Performance 2030**, alternatively **Asset Performance as a Service (APaaS)**.

**Strategic thesis (hypothesis, to be pressure-tested, not asserted as fact):** Bilfinger should continue evolving along the value chain — labour provider → maintenance provider → integrated maintenance partner → reliability partner → asset management partner → asset performance partner → outcome-based strategic partner — on the premise that the next competitive advantage is not cheaper maintenance but measurable industrial performance.

**Important grounding fact (see `14_RESEARCH/BILFINGER_FACTS.md`):** Bilfinger has already publicly adopted the tagline "Your Performance Is Our Business" as part of its 2030 strategy, and already operates a named digital platform, BCAP® (Bilfinger Connected Asset Performance). This project's job is therefore **not** to introduce the idea of performance-orientation to Bilfinger — Bilfinger has already said it out loud. The job is to show a credible, de-risked, staged **commercial and contractual mechanism** for turning that slogan into an actual outcome-based revenue architecture, informed by lived experience on both sides of that kind of contract. That is the differentiated contribution Petri can credibly claim; positioning it as a novel strategic insight would not survive a CEO-level challenge.

## The twelve questions this concept must answer

1. What customer problem are we solving?
2. Why is the traditional maintenance commercial model insufficient?
3. Where is the economic value pool?
4. What part of performance can Bilfinger credibly influence?
5. What must remain the customer's responsibility?
6. How should performance attribution work?
7. How should contract risk be limited?
8. What is the commercial model?
9. How does Bilfinger make higher-quality earnings?
10. Why can Bilfinger credibly deliver this?
11. How can the concept be piloted?
12. How can it be scaled across customers and countries?

See `STRATEGIC_THESIS.md` for the first-cycle answers, and `WORKPLAN.md` for which of these are still thin and need another pass.
