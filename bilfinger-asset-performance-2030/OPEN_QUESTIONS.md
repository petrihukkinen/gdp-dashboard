## For Petri — positioning and judgment calls

2. **[CYCLE-1 PRIORITY]** Do you have any visibility (from the recruitment process, from public sources, or from your own network) into whether Bilfinger already runs any KPI-linked or gainshare contracts anywhere in the group? 3. **[CYCLE-1 PRIORITY]** In your ABB experience moving ~43 contracts from hourly to fixed/performance models, what was the single biggest commercial risk that actually materialized (not the risk you feared most, but the one that actually bit)? 4. **[CYCLE-1 PRIORITY]** At Neste, was there ever an internal or contractor dispute over whether a loss event was "maintenance-caused" vs. "operations-caused"? How was it actually resolved in practice?
