## Corporate profile

- Bilfinger SE, German industrial services group, headquartered in Mannheim, listed on Frankfurt Stock Exchange (SDAX).
- ~24,990 employees group-wide as of Dec 2025 (up from 24,478 in 2023). Largest workforce concentrations: UK (~20.6%), Germany (~12.7%), Netherlands (~9.3%). [Revelio Labs employee data, aggregated from public/LinkedIn sources — treat as directional, not audited]
- Core industries served: energy, chemicals & petrochemicals, oil & gas, pharma & biopharma, and (per some pages) metallurgy. Primary geographies: Europe, North America, Middle East.
- Segment structure through FY2025: three geography-based segments — Western Europe, Central Europe, International. **Bilfinger has stated it is realigning its segment structure starting in 2026** for "greater proximity to the market" — the new structure was not fully detailed in the sources found. ## Strategy: "Bilfinger 2030 — Your Performance Is Our Business"

Bilfinger published an updated strategy (2026) under the tagline **"Your Performance Is Our Business."** This is directly relevant — Bilfinger has already claimed the "performance" positioning at the corporate-brand level, which is both a validation and a constraint for this project (see STRATEGIC_THESIS.md — we cannot present "asset performance partner" as a novel insight; it has to out-execute an existing corporate slogan).

2030 targets stated in the strategy update:
- Average annual revenue growth of 8–10%, organic + inorganic (M&A).
- EBITA margin target raised to 8–9% (from 5.5% in FY2025 — a material step-up).
- Cash conversion rate of at least 90% sustained to 2030.

Two stated strategic levers:
1. **Operational Excellence** — continuous improvement of Bilfinger's own execution/delivery.
2. **Market Expansion** — deeper customer focus, positioning as "preferred partner for efficiency and sustainability."

CEO quote (Dr. Thomas Schulz, Group CEO): "Our updated strategy underlines our ambition to be the number 1 in enhancing our customers' efficiency and sustainability in volatile markets."

**Strategic reading (hypothesis, not fact):** an EBITA margin ambition of 8–9% by 2030 (vs. 5.5% today) cannot be reached through labour-rate service delivery alone at this scale — it structurally requires either (a) a materially richer service/digital mix, (b) disciplined portfolio/bidding selectivity, or (c) M&A into higher-margin adjacencies, most plausibly all three. This is the single best internal argument for why Bilfinger's own board should already be receptive to an Asset Performance / outcome-based concept — it is one of the few credible levers to move a group-wide services business from ~5.5% to 8–9% EBITA. This needs to be validated with Bilfinger's own investor materials (capital markets day slides), not asserted as settled fact.

## Existing digital / "asset performance" capability

Bilfinger already has real product infrastructure in this space — this project must build on it, not duplicate or contradict it:

- **BCAP® (Bilfinger Connected Asset Performance)** — a named, existing product. Cloud platform combining process control system, ERP, and CMMS data; features include Asset Monitoring (sensor dashboards), Smart Alerting (AI-filtered alarms), predictive analytics. Sold as multi-year subscriptions tied to plant size / number of connected assets (3–5 year contracts typical). Pilots typically start on one production line / rotating-equipment subset before scaling.
- **Bilfinger Maintenance Intelligence** — predictive analytics + standardized maintenance workflows, pitched at refineries/chemical sites with fragmented data.
- **Bilfinger Maintenance Concept (BMC)** — standardized, modular maintenance framework (asset strategy, planning/scheduling, materials management, performance measurement, org development as combinable modules). Marketed claim: up to 40% cost reduction over 5 years and up to 10% productivity increase "under certain conditions" (unverified methodology — treat as vendor marketing claim, not audited result).
- Case study language references BCAP improving product quality rate / reducing waste for a customer (Almatis, per search snippet — needs primary confirmation).

**Important implication:** "Bilfinger Connected Asset Performance" is already Bilfinger's own brand name — dangerously close to the working term "Asset Performance as a Service (APaaS)" used in this project. This is a naming collision risk (see RISK_REGISTER.md) and also an opportunity: the new concept can be positioned as the *commercial and contractual* evolution of BCAP (moving BCAP's outputs from a monitoring subscription into a KPI-linked outcome fee), rather than a competing platform name.

## What was NOT found (gaps — do not assume)

- No confirmed public case study of Bilfinger running a **KPI-linked or gainshare/painshare commercial contract** with a named customer. Search turned up only generic performance-contracting literature (Simon-Kucher, ScienceDirect, PHTI), not a Bilfinger-specific example. **Do not claim Bilfinger already does this — treat outcome-based commercial models as a gap in Bilfinger's current portfolio, to be confirmed or refuted in due diligence with insiders.**
- No confirmed detail on the 2026 segment realignment beyond the fact that it is happening.
- No confirmed org chart / named leadership below CEO level for the relevant service lines (Maintenance, Modifications & Turnarounds) — needed before a 100-day plan can be realistic about sponsors and internal allies.
- No confirmed data on Bilfinger's current EBITA-by-service-line split (i.e., how much of the 5.5% blended margin comes from maintenance vs. engineering/turnarounds vs. digital) — this materially affects whether "digital/performance" is really the highest-margin lever or a rounding error today.
