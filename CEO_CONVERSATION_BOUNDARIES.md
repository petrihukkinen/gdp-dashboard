# CEO Conversation Boundaries — Cycle 9

For a 15-minute conversation with a Bilfinger-scale CEO-level executive. AMOR 2030 must be
positioned as **a strategically developed hypothesis, grounded in direct industrial
experience and eight cycles of commercial stress-testing — not a finished strategy.**

## Five claims currently too weak to state as conclusions

1. "Customers will pay a separate fee for Control Tower orchestration." Unvalidated —
   `FALSIFICATION_REGISTER.md` #1.
2. "This creates a defensible competitive moat." `DIFFERENTIATION_FALSIFICATION.md`'s
   verdict is UNRESOLVED, not confirmed.
3. "The commercial model is ready to price and sell." Real numbers (Base Fee, splits,
   thresholds) remain deliberately deferred through Cycle 8.
4. "This exceeds standard industry liability/incentive terms." Explicitly withdrawn in
   Cycle 8 — no benchmark evidence exists anywhere in this project.
5. "The organization will adopt this without internal resistance." Untested —
   `FALSIFICATION_REGISTER.md` #4, who-sells/who-owns-customer risk entirely unexamined
   with real stakeholders.

## Five claims strong enough to state

1. The core architecture has survived eight cycles of adversarial internal stress-testing
   without a single Red Team scenario proving fatal — unusually well-examined for a
   hypothesis at this stage, though "well-examined" is not "externally validated."
2. Two major components (Event 2 entirely; Event 3 in every commercial form tested) were
   deliberately killed after rigorous testing rather than preserved to protect sunk design
   effort — a real discipline signal about how this work was done.
3. Petri's direct, dual-sided experience — provider-side fixed-price/performance transition
   at ABB, customer-side asset management and contractor-integration design at Neste/AMOR —
   is real Level A/B evidence supporting the *general direction* (industrial services moving
   from time-and-materials toward fixed-price/performance-linked models), even though it
   does not prove every specific mechanism built on top of it (see
   `PETRI_ARCHITECTURE_EVIDENCE_MAP.md`).
4. The architecture explicitly separates provider-controlled from customer-dependent value,
   using corrected, non-overclaiming language ("primarily provider-controlled, with defined
   customer dependencies," never "fully controlled") — a level of intellectual honesty most
   commercial pitches don't carry.
5. The specific evidence needed to validate or kill the model's riskiest assumptions has
   been identified and written down before asking for any commercial commitment — Petri can
   honestly say "here is exactly what I still need to learn from you," a mature and credible
   opening posture, not a weakness.

## Three questions Petri should ask the CEO

Designed to turn the conversation itself into the next falsification step, not to seek
approval prematurely.

1. *"If a provider offered to take formal orchestration accountability for your entire
   industrial services ecosystem — not just delivery, but coordination and consequence —
   would that be commercially interesting to your organization as a distinct, separately
   priced capability, or would you expect it bundled into normal service pricing?"*
   (Tests Falsification Register #1/#9 at the CEO level directly.)
2. *"Where in your organization would resistance to a model like this most likely come
   from — and would you personally sponsor working through that resistance if the customer
   economics were proven?"*
   (Tests Falsification Register #4 with the one person who could actually resolve it.)
3. *"What would you need to see — real customer evidence, a pilot, a specific number —
   before you'd consider this worth a deeper strategic commitment?"*
   (Converts the meeting into a falsification step rather than a premature ask.)

## Positioning discipline
Do not present AMOR 2030 as finished. Do not lead with the Control Tower differentiation
claim as settled. Lead with the problem (fragmented accountability, unmeasured performance
economics), the discipline of the process (eight cycles, real kills, no protected sunk
work), and the specific open questions — not with conclusions the evidence doesn't yet
support.
