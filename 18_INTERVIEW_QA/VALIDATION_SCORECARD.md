- Score each hypothesis independently — a strong result on one does not upgrade another.

  SUPPORTED.** Minimum sample per `CUSTOMER_CFO_VALIDATION.md`/`CUSTOMER_ASSET_DIRECTOR_VALIDATION.md`/
  `PROVIDER_EXECUTIVE_VALIDATION.md` is 3-5 respondents per category, across 2-3 distinct
  customer contexts, per Cycle 8's Task 10 design.
- A FALSIFIED critical hypothesis (#1-5) should trigger an immediate return to this project
  before any further commercial development, not a narrative workaround.
- This scorecard is completed once real interviews occur. **No entries exist yet — nothing
