# Zero-Based Control Tower Organization — Cycle 11

Starting from zero dedicated FTE. A role is added only when a specific accountability
requirement cannot credibly be delivered through the existing delivery organization.

| Role | Mandatory/Optional | Accountability requirement | Decision owned | Workload driver | Existing org can perform? | Shareable across sites? | Centralizable? | Automatable? | Consequence if removed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Contract Director** | Mandatory | Overall commercial/relationship accountability, escalation | Commercial positions, escalation resolution | Relationship intensity, roughly site-count-independent | Partially — a strong account manager in any large contract already does most of this | Only for smaller accounts | No | No | No single accountable owner — undermines the whole bounded-accountability claim |
| **Site Manager** | Mandatory | Day-to-day delivery, safety, workforce | Daily execution, on-site resourcing | Site headcount/scope | **Yes — predates AMOR entirely** | No | No | No | No delivery leadership at all |
| **Asset Performance Lead** | Conditional | KPI Pyramid maintenance, Event 4 evidence packaging | None independently — analysis/reporting support | Number of performance events + KPI cadence | Partially — reliability/planning staff do adjacent work already | **Yes** | Partially (methodology, not application) | Partially | No credible input to Value Validation Board |
| **Reliability Lead** | Optional | Reliability engineering discipline | RCA/FMEA execution | Asset criticality, failure frequency | **Yes, largely — standard good practice in any competent maintenance org** | Methods only | Methods only | No | Reduced reliability discipline, but not an AMOR-specific loss |
| **Work Management Lead** | Mandatory | Planning/scheduling discipline | Work sequencing | Work-order volume | **Yes — standard function, not AMOR-specific at all** | No | No | Partially | Poor execution discipline, independent of AMOR |
| **Commercial Controller** | Mandatory | Contract financial administration, VVB commercial support | Invoicing, financial reconciliation | Transaction/claim volume | Largely yes, with a modest incremental VVB-support component | **Yes, highly** | **Yes, highly** | Partially | No credible financial governance |
| **Data Analyst** | Conditional | Module-level attribution data support | None independently | Only material where a module is active | No — this is genuinely incremental where needed | **Yes, highly** | **Yes, highly** | **Yes, significant** | No module can be credibly operated |
| **Digital Specialist** | Optional | CMMS/data integration | None independently | Data-system fragmentation | Often yes, or vendor-sourced | Yes | Yes | Partially | Manual data reconciliation persists — a cost, not a failure |
| **Contractor Integration Lead** | **Mandatory where genuine orchestration authority is exercised** | Day-to-day direction of the multi-contractor ecosystem, cross-contractor accountability | Contractor sequencing, escalation among parties | Number of contractors orchestrated | Partially — subcontract admin exists today, but *formal* orchestration authority is qualitatively more | **No — needs real-time site presence** | No | No | **The single clearest genuine incremental AMOR cost — without it, "orchestration" is a claim, not a capability** |
| **Value Validation support** | Event-driven | Evidence processing for VVB claims | None independently | Number of adjudicated events (should be low by design) | No, but should be lean | **Yes, highly** | **Yes, highly** | Partially | Slower, less rigorous claim adjudication |

## Finding
Of ten roles tested, only **two** are genuinely mandatory, dedicated, and site-specific as
pure incremental AMOR cost: **Contract Director** (partially existing) and **Contractor
Integration Lead** (the clearest, least reducible incremental cost). Every other role is
either (a) already existing work under a different name, (b) shareable/centralizable, (c)
conditional on module activation, or (d) event-driven and should stay lean. The "Asset
Performance Office" implied by earlier cycles' governance sketches (`08_OPERATING_MODEL/README.md`)
is considerably smaller in its genuinely incremental form than a full named-capability list
would suggest.
