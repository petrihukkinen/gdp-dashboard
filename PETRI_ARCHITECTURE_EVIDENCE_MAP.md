# Petri Experience → Architecture Evidence Map — Cycle 9

Mapping Petri's direct experience against the current settled architecture. Three
categories: **PROVEN BY DIRECT EXPERIENCE**, **STRONGLY INFORMED BY EXPERIENCE**, **NEW
HYPOTHESIS**. Written to be honest even where the finding is uncomfortable.

| Architecture element | Category | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Base Fee / fixed-price delivery model | **PROVEN BY DIRECT EXPERIENCE** | ABB — ~43 Finnish Full Service contracts moved to fixed-price delivery (`PETRI_EXPERIENCE.md` B1) |
| Performance/OEE-linked commercial logic (general concept) | **STRONGLY INFORMED BY EXPERIENCE** | Petri confirms ABB used OEE/production-performance linkage in commercial logic (Level B) — but this supports the *general direction*, not the specific atomic-event/attribution-engine mechanics built in this project on top of it |
| Contractor consolidation / integrator role | **PROVEN BY DIRECT EXPERIENCE (as a designed, customer-endorsed concept) — NOT proven as an executed mechanism** | AMOR 2020's explicit integrator design (`EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` A13) — a real customer-side proposal Neste built and endorsed internally — but AMOR 2020 was never executed (B4), so this is proven as a credible design with real buy-in, not as something that worked in practice |
| Downside/painshare corridor (Event 4) with real financial consequence | **NEW HYPOTHESIS** | Neither AMOR 2020 (no painshare mechanism at all, per the KPI set in A9) nor ABB's described Full Service contracts include a capped, provider-funded painshare mechanism resembling Event 4. **This is worth stating plainly: Event 4 — the element Cycle 8 found to be the sole thing preventing regression to "Better Managed Maintenance" — has the weakest direct evidentiary support of any major architecture element.** |
| Control Tower Economics — orchestration priced as a distinct commercial capability | **NEW HYPOTHESIS** | AMOR 2020 built the *shape* of an integrator role but never priced orchestration as a separate line item — it was folded into the same markup/fixed-fee structure as delivery (A7/A8). ABB's experience doesn't include an orchestration-specific fee either. This is a genuinely new commercial idea, not something either direct experience proves. |
| Joint governance / Value Validation Board with parity voting | **NEW HYPOTHESIS** | AMOR 2020's governance was trust-based and informal (`STRATEGIC_THESIS.md`'s finding on founder-level relationship reliance); nothing in either ABB or AMOR resembles a formal, parity-voted joint board adjudicating payment claims. |
| Contractor Productivity Value Module — bounded, time-limited share design | **STRONGLY INFORMED, not proven** | ABB proves fixed-price/performance-linked pricing is achievable and real. It does not prove the specific bounded/time-limited-share mechanic invented in Cycle 8 to solve the perpetual-rent problem — that specific mechanic has no precedent in either data source and is untested anywhere. |
| Customer willingness to cede orchestration decision rights | **NEW HYPOTHESIS — arguably contradicted by the strongest available evidence** | AMOR 2020 (Level A) shows Neste retained significant control even under trust-based, founder-led conditions: budget approval rights, spares ownership, a reversion clause on termination (A3, A4, A7). If anything, the best real evidence available suggests customers retain *more* control than the current architecture's decision-rights assumptions might expect, not less. This is flagged directly in `FALSIFICATION_REGISTER.md` #3 and should be treated as a live risk, not smoothed over. |

## What this table means for the CEO conversation
The two architecture elements most load-bearing for AMOR 2030's actual differentiation —
**Event 4's downside corridor** and **Control Tower Economics as a priced capability** — are
**both new hypotheses**, not proven by either of Petri's two direct experience bases. This
does not mean they are wrong. It means they cannot be presented as validated by experience,
and any claim that implies otherwise would overstate what this project's evidence actually
supports. See `CEO_CONVERSATION_BOUNDARIES.md`.
