`05_VALUE_POOLS/`, `business_model_options.md`) against: customer value, commercial
credibility, controllability, attribution, financial logic, scalability, differentiation,

## Checker's overall verdict
Cycle 1 output is disciplined rather than impressive — it deliberately narrows the
commercially defensible story rather than repeating AMOR 2020's inflated-potential pattern.
That is the correct call for Gate 1/2, even though it produces a smaller, less exciting
headline number than the protocol's full seven-rung ladder implies. Recommend Petri see this
framed explicitly as a strength, not a limitation, when presented.
