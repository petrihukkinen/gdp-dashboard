## Resolved (2026-07-11, by Petri's direct answers)

**Q1 — What happened to the 2020 AMOR proposal?** RESOLVED: it was not executed. No AMOR Oy
was formed. Treat all its figures as unvalidated proposal targets, never as achieved
results. See `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` B4.

**Q2 — Who is the acting party for AMOR 2030?** RESOLVED (as working context): a
Bilfinger-scale industrial services provider, developed in the context of Petri's real
senior leadership discussions with Bilfinger. Do not invent specific Bilfinger capabilities
beyond this. See `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` B5.

**Q3 — Refining-only or broader?** RESOLVED: broader asset-intensive continuous/
semi-continuous process industries (refining, chemicals, petrochemicals, energy-intensive
process industries, selected pulp & paper, selected metals & minerals). Not all industrial
sectors. See `ASSUMPTIONS.md` AS5.

**Q5 — Preserve MBO/founder-trust governance, or move to a platform model?** RESOLVED: move
fully to a process-driven, replicable platform model. Personal leadership trust stays
important but is not the operating model. See `ASSUMPTIONS.md` AS6, `DECISION_LOG.md`.

**Q4 — Which number is credible: €43m committed or €24-62m potential?** PARTIALLY RESOLVED.
Petri: treat €43m as an unvalidated 2020 proposal target, not evidence. Adopt the formula
**Identified Value × Controllability × Realization Probability × Time-to-Impact = Committable
Value** going forward (refines the protocol's Section 15 formula for this project). The
remaining work — actually applying this formula to real value pools — is Cycle 2's job, see
`07_KPI_ATTRIBUTION/attribution_architecture.md`.
