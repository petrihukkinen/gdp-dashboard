# Project Charter — AMOR 2030

## Owner
Petri Hukkinen

## Origin
AMOR (Asset Management One Refinery) — a 2020 management buy-out proposal at Neste's One
Refinery (Porvoo/Naantali), authored by Petri Hukkinen, Jari Ala-Nikkola, Mika Raassina and
Juha Kekkonen. Full source in `01_SOURCE_MATERIAL/`. AMOR 2020 is historical source
material and evidence of a working pattern — it is not the answer, and its slides are not to
be re-skinned.

## Strategic question
Can a large industrial services company create a scalable, higher-value business model by
moving from maintenance delivery towards measurable asset performance outcomes?

## Working thesis (Level C — to be tested, not assumed true)
"The next competitive advantage is not cheaper maintenance. It is measurable industrial
performance." See `STRATEGIC_THESIS.md` and `HYPOTHESIS_REGISTER.md`.

## Standard of work
Every deliverable must be discussable with a Bilfinger-caliber CEO, Executive Board, CFO, a
division CEO, a refinery/chemical-site executive, and a private equity operating partner —
i.e. it survives a skeptical CFO and a skeptical customer, not just a friendly read.

## What this project is not
- Not a PowerPoint production exercise.
- Not a validation service for Petri's ideas — the mandate is to test and, if warranted,
  break the thesis.
- Not a CV or job-application document.
- Not an AI showcase — AI is enabler-only and must pass the AI Value Test to appear anywhere.

## Gate structure
See `WORKPLAN.md` for the 10-gate structure (Gate 0 Source Integrity → Gate 9 100-Day Plan)
and current status. No executive deck is produced before Gate 3 (Commercial Viability).

## Evidence discipline
Every claim is tagged Level A (verified fact), Level B (Petri's direct experience), or
Level C (hypothesis) in `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md`. Nothing crosses from C to A by repetition.
